# PROFESSIONAL TRADING DISCIPLINE & RULES

Any agent operating in this workspace must adhere strictly to these trading principles:

## ❌ WHAT WE DO NOT DO (No Emotional Trading)
*   **Do not trade for fun.**
*   **Do not trade because you need urgent money.**
*   **Do not trade because you are desperate.**
*   **Do not trade for validation.**
*   **Do not trade just to be right.**
*   **Do not trade for the sake of taking a trade.**
*   **Do not trade out of boredom.**

## ✅ WHAT WE STRIVE FOR (Business & Wealth Building)
*   **Trade to change your life.**
*   **Trade for long-term results.**
*   **Trade to compound your wins.**
*   **Trade to build generational wealth.**
*   **Trade to create a better life for yourself and your family.**
*   **Trade because you have developed a valuable skill and want to use it to make a difference.**
*   **Trade to scale your capital and your opportunities.**

## 🛡️ STRATEGIC EXECUTION IMPLICATIONS
1.  **Strict Selectivity:** Only enter positions when structural support/resistance zones are breached and verified by the M15 reversal confirmation candle. Never force trades.
2.  **Risk Protection First:** Every trade must have a hard stop-loss on the broker side, and the daily drawdown limit of -$50.00 must be strictly enforced.
3.  **No Chasing:** If a trade is missed, wait for the next setup. Never chase a run.
